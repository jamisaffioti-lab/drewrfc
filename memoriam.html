Remembering our brothers and sisters who have passed.

Home› In Memoriam

Having been around now for over 60 years, DRFC (and DWRFC) have lost many former players. Below is the list constructed by numerous alums from all eras. While the list is sadly long, it’s definitely incomplete.

You can submit others here. Please include as much information as you know. We have entered information for some of those listed. Information will be added for the rest, assuming there’s information available online. Please contact us if you have additions or details.
